Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 2, 2026

Jomhoor is designed with privacy as a core principle. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when users use the Jomhoor mobile application and related services.

1. Information processed during verification

The Jomhoor app may use the device camera, NFC functionality, and approximate location access to complete user verification and provide app functionality.

During the verification process, the app may temporarily process information from an identity document, NFC chip, facial image, or device sensors. This information is used only to complete the verification process.

2. Local processing

Identity information and verification images are processed locally on the user's device. Jomhoor does not store identity document images, facial images, or extracted identity information on its servers.

3. Deletion after verification

After the verification process is completed, identity information and verification images are permanently deleted from the app, including from the user's device. The app does not keep copies of the user's identity document or facial image after verification.

4. Information we do not collect

Jomhoor does not sell user data. Jomhoor does not use identity document images or facial images for advertising. Jomhoor does not store users' identity documents or facial images on backend servers.

5. Approximate location

The app may request approximate location access to support location-based app functionality. Precise location is not required. The approximate location is anonymized by adding a random offset.

6. Camera and NFC access

Camera access may be used to scan documents or complete verification steps. NFC access may be used to read supported identity document chips. These permissions are used only for app functionality and verification.

7. Data security

Jomhoor is designed to minimize data collection and reduce the amount of sensitive information that leaves the user's device. Where communication with backend services is required, reasonable technical safeguards are used to protect transmitted data.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact us at: info@jomhoor.org
